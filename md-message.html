What steers the group

Vision & Values

The direction, the standards and the programmes that keep L&W raising its own bar.

The words we work by

Raising the bar, then raising it again.

“Yesterday's excellence is today's standard and tomorrow's mediocrity.”

A standard we hold ourselves to

Vision

To be the leading and preferred contractor and business partner in India and the region, and to be recognized as the employer of choice.

Mission

To consistently improve and deliver excellent quality and reliable products and services with integrity, to our valued clients, by our team of trained, competent and responsible staff.

Values

Safety. Integrity. Quality. Teamwork. Innovation. Growth.

The programmes behind the words

A vision only counts if something concrete carries it. At L&W, three group-wide programmes do.

Build to Grow. Cultural alignment is trained, not assumed. In 2019 the group invested in a modern residential technical and training centre in Bengaluru, and close to 2,500 man-days of cultural training have been conducted there so far.

HYPHEN. A digital platform built specifically for L&W, bringing the group's applications together so that project sites and support functions collaborate on the same live information. ERP and HRMS systems run across the company as it moves towards a paperless, agile way of working.

Sustainable development. Mobile apps at every project site capture, and keep visible, the daily consumption and conservation of natural resources, with a holistic sustainability policy being put in place to govern these efforts.

At group level, the subsidiaries let customers hand over the whole brief: L&W Building Solutions for interiors, landscape and home building; Invreco for precast, structural steel, M&E, façade, and plant and machinery; L&W Realty for property development.
