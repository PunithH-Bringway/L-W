From the Managing Director

MD's Message

Mr. Asaithambi Manickam on culture, digitalisation and building a group that keeps raising its own bar.

A letter to our partners

Raising the bar, then raising it again.

“The world around us is rapidly changing and bringing newer opportunities as well as challenges at unprecedented pace. To be successful, it is important we continue capitalizing on the new opportunities and cope with the challenges.”

Mr. Asaithambi Manickam, Managing Director

Hello!

The world is changing quickly, bringing opportunities and challenges at a pace none of us have seen before. To succeed, we must keep seizing the new opportunities and manage the challenges well.

In today's world, “Yesterday's excellence is today's standard and tomorrow's mediocrity.” It has never mattered more to keep learning, and to pick up new skills quickly, so that our standard of performance stays high.

I am pleased to share updates on some of our key initiatives.

L&W is focused on cultural alignment through our Build to Grow programme. In 2019 we invested in a modern residential technical and training centre, where we have conducted close to 2,500 man-days of cultural training.

We are equally committed to digitalisation. We have launched HYPHEN, a digital platform built specifically for L&W, which brings several digital applications together so that project sites and support functions collaborate on the same live information. ERP and HRMS systems now run across the company as we move towards a paperless and agile organisation.

As a responsible contractor, L&W is committed to sustainable development. We have launched mobile apps at all project sites to capture, and keep visible, the daily consumption and conservation of natural resources, and we are putting a holistic sustainability policy in place to govern these efforts.

At group level, we have expanded our subsidiaries so that customers can hand us the whole brief. L&W Building Solutions covers interiors, landscape and home building. Invreco carries the specialised works: precast, structural steel, M&E, façade, plant and machinery. L&W Realty develops property.

I am confident these initiatives and investments will play a significant part in our customers' success, deepen employee engagement and cultural alignment within L&W, and improve our operating metrics through greater effectiveness and efficiency.

Warm regards,

Mr. Asaithambi Manickam
Managing Director, L&W Group
