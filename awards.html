Recognition

Awards

Certifications and honours held across L&W Construction and its Singapore parents, Woh Hup and Lee Kim Tah.

India

L&W Construction Private Limited

  • Bureau Veritas Certification – ISO 9001 (QMS), ISO 14001 (EMS), ISO 45001 (OHSMS) Management Systems
  • British Safety Council – International Safety Award
  • Construction Industry Development Council – Vishwakarma Award
  • National Safety Council – Prashamsa Suraksha, Puraskara Award
  • National Safety Council – Suraksha Puraskar Award
  • Karnataka State Safety Institute – Safety Day Award
  • Building and Construction Authority, Singapore – SGBuilds Award (SGBA), SGBuilds Star Award (SGBSA)

Singapore

Woh Hup Holdings Private Limited

Building since the 1920s, Woh Hup has collected 316 International Safety Awards and 58 BCA Construction Excellence Awards, and was the first contractor to win the Quality Star Champion award.

  • BCA Green & Gracious Builder Award
  • BCA Built Environment Leadership Award
  • People Developer (PD)
  • International Federation of Asian & Western Pacific Contractors' Associations (IFAWPCA)
  • BCA Green Mark Awards
  • BCA Best Buildable Design Awards
  • BCA Construction Excellence Awards
  • BCA ISO 9001/ISO 14001/OHSMS 18001 and Best Practice Awards
  • Safety Awards
  • CONQUAS Certificates

Singapore

Lee Kim Tah Private Limited

A builder since the 1920s, and among the first few construction companies listed on the Singapore Exchange.

  • Construction Industry Development Board's Award for "Construction Excellence 1995" – Rasa Sentosa Beach Resort Project
  • "Best Buildable Design 1992" (Residential Buildings > 200 Units) – Pasir Ris N1C6
  • "Construction Excellence 1991" – Lilac Park
  • "Construction Quality Assessment Certificate 1991" – Lilac Park (CONQUAS score 80.8)
  • "Certificate of Merit for Construction Excellence 1989" – Geylang West C1 Project
  • "Accident-Free Award" (1988/1989) – Pasir Ris N4C1 and N1C6
  • "Construction Excellence 1988" – Nims Garden Project
  • Gold Award "Most Safety-Conscious Worksite" (1984, 1985)
  • Gold Award "Most Productive Worksite" (1983, 1985)
  • Gold Award from IFAWPCA (1978) for "Excellence in Construction Performance"
