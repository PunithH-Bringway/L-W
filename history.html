Since 2006

History & Milestones

The full record. Every milestone from our founding in India in 2006 to the marquee wins of 2023, year by year, project by project.

The complete timeline

From one office in Bengaluru to a national footprint.

What follows is the whole story: every regional office opened, every subsidiary incorporated, and every project milestone on the record since day one.

2006

Where it begins.

  • L&W founded in India

2008

The first construction contracts arrive, and the order book opens across Tamil Nadu and beyond.

  • Ascendas One Hub Coimbatore
  • Krishil Botanica Condominiums
  • ITP Chennai Ph 3

2010

A permanent base in Chennai, and the quality system formalised.

  • Opening of Regional Office in Chennai
  • ISO Certification

2011

Residential in Bengaluru, industrial in Chennai.

  • 27 Park Avenue Bangalore
  • Milton Roy India Chennai

2013

Ten milestones in one year, with marquee names across Bengaluru and Chennai.

  • One Hub Chennai Infrastructure Development
  • Tyco Bangalore
  • Amada Bangalore
  • Global Technology Ph 1 Bangalore
  • RMZ Eco World 4A, 4B, 4C Bangalore
  • Citadine Bangalore
  • Home 3 Bangalore
  • Shindengen Bangalore
  • TRMN Bangalore
  • Ajinomoto Chennai

2014

The corporate office opens in Bengaluru and the group pushes west into Pune.

  • Opening of Corporate Office in Bangalore
  • Opening of Regional Office in Pune
  • ITP Pune Ph 2
  • Qualcomm-C
  • ITP Bangalore MTB Ph 3
  • NetApp Bangalore
  • Takasago Chennai

2015

Hyderabad joins the map, and the first subsidiary is incorporated.

  • Opening of Regional Office in Hyderabad
  • Incorporation of L&W Building Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
  • 'V' IT Park Hyderabad
  • Qualcomm Data Centre Bangalore

2017

The tenth anniversary year, and the busiest to that point: fifteen milestones on the record.

  • L&W 10th Anniversary
  • Global Technology Ph 2 Bangalore
  • ITP Gurgaon Ph 1
  • Capgemini-IGATE Ph 2 Mumbai
  • East Point Residential
  • Honeywell Campus Ph 2 Bangalore
  • ITP Pune Ph 3
  • ITPL Bangalore
  • RMZ One Paramount Chennai
  • Capgemini Pune
  • KMI Penukonda
  • TCPL Pune
  • ITP Bangalore MTB Ph 4
  • KMI Steel Structure Penukonda
  • KMI Assembly Penukonda

2018

Three new companies incorporated in a single year, alongside fourteen project milestones from Boeing to White Alpha.

  • Incorporation of Invreco Pvt. Ltd.
  • Incorporation of LWK Development Pvt. Ltd.
  • Gateway Office Park Chennai
  • Tech Mahindra Pune
  • TCL Chennai
  • Wistron Bangalore
  • TCL Pune
  • Boeing Bangalore
  • ITP Pune Ph 4 Pune
  • KMI Township Penukonda
  • Futura Chennai
  • RMZ Nexity Hyderabad
  • RMZ Spire Hyderabad
  • ITP Pune Kharadi
  • Qualcomm F&N Bangalore
  • White Alpha Bangalore

2020

The realty arm is born, and two more marquee names join the books.

  • Incorporation of L&W Realty Pvt. Ltd.
  • Hewlett Packard Bangalore
  • Radial IT Park Chennai

2021

Two more mandates, including a data centre for one of India's largest conglomerates.

  • Bangalore Tower Private Limited
  • Adani Data Center Chennai

2023

Five milestones across three cities, capped by Provenance Land in Mumbai.

  • One Hub Chennai
  • AIGP Block 2 Pune
  • Vector Pune
  • Fern Mumbai
  • Provenance Land Mumbai
