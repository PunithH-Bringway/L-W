Moments that mattered

Events

Openings, launches and milestones from across the L&W group, recorded as they happened.

Inauguration · November 2022

L&W inaugurates its Mumbai regional office

Mumbai holds high potential for L&W, and the group was already executing projects in the region. To support those project teams, and to prepare for further expansion, the L&W Group Mumbai Regional Office was set up at Vikhroli West.

The opening ceremony was held on 28 November 2022. Mr. Kim Yong, Chairman of the Woh Hup Group of Companies, Singapore, performed the inauguration together with Mr. Asaithambi, Managing Director of the L&W Group of Companies, India, and Mr. Edmund Cheah, Director of the Lee Kim Tah Group of Companies, Singapore. Clients, partners, business associates and L&W staff joined them and made it a grand occasion.

Opening · May 2019

Technology & Innovation Centre opens in Bengaluru

L&W launched a Technology & Innovation Centre along with a modern residential Training Academy in Bengaluru, Karnataka. The academy provides need-based training to employees at every level, so that each of them is competent and well equipped for their professional responsibilities: leadership development, technical training, skill upgradation and soft skills.

Training at the centre is designed and conducted with an integrated approach, keeping L&W's objectives and values at its core. Every programme is enabled, administered and monitored for effectiveness through an integrated Learning Management System (LMS).

The campus offers:

  • Eminence Block: training space for 150 professionals
  • Emphasis Block: collaborative working space for 150 professionals
  • Habitat: living space for 57 professionals
  • Conference rooms
  • Recreational and gym facilities

Launch · 14 November 2019

HYPHEN, the group's integrated digital platform, goes live

HYPHEN is the L&W corporate intranet: the single home where all of the group's digital applications come to life, with access for every employee and key partner.

Mr. Asaithambi Manickam, Managing Director, launched HYPHEN digitally on 14 November 2019 at the L&W Technology & Innovation Centre, Bengaluru. The entire programme was made live through streaming, and staff at project sites, the head office and the regional offices joined in from their own locations.

Retreat · October 2019

Another year of excellence: the management retreat

The L&W Group of Companies management retreat was held on 12 and 13 October 2019 at Angsana Resort, Bengaluru. Three focus areas set the agenda: the digitalization rollout, the ARM update on revenue and targets, and Build to Grow training.

Programme · March 2019

L&W accelerates digital adoption

Our mission is to accelerate technology adoption and put relevant technologies to work in a smarter way. The goal is to address the change holistically, from three perspectives at once:

  • People: cultural transformation towards a high-performance organization, through standardized work methods, effective training and collective learning
  • Process: streamlining and removing wasteful process steps by adopting LEAN methodologies
  • Technology: flexible, relevant technologies that automate the streamlined processes, with systems that bring the teams together
