Services · Specialist works

Invreco

The specialist works arm of L&W, incorporated in 2018: facade, MEP, structural steel, precast, and plant & machinery.

01 · Facade

Engineering the building's face.

From design to construction and post-completion assessment, our team takes an integrated approach to every aspect of facade engineering. Five systems cover the full envelope:

  • Unitized curtain wall system
  • Semi unitized curtain wall system
  • ACP cladding works
  • Spider glazing and fin glass system
  • Stone cladding

02 · MEP

Mechanical, electrical & plumbing.

L&W undertakes a wide range of mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, keeping the systems behind the architecture in the same hands as the structure itself.

  • Piping
  • Pipe insulation
  • Ducting
  • Duct insulation
  • Installation of air terminals

03 · Structural steel

Fabricated in Devanahalli,
erected in sequence.

Fabrication runs at our Devanahalli factory under a covered area of approximately 30,000 sq ft, which keeps quality and safety fully under control. Present capacity stands at 200 Mt a month.

After fabrication, every member is painted to the project specification, then loaded onto trailers carrying part mark numbers in the exact sequence of erection and sent to site. What arrives is ready to go up, in order.

  • Metal structures
  • Deck slab
  • Temporary support structures
  • Archie structures
  • PEB structures
30,000Sq ft covered fabrication area, approx.
200Mt fabricated per month at present

04 · Precast

Cast in Chennai, finished on site.

Precast concrete is produced by casting concrete in a reusable mould, curing it in a controlled environment, transporting it to the construction site and lifting it into place. Our manufacturing facility is in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

The service does not stop at manufacture. We also transport, lift, install and finish every element we cast.

  • Parapet wall
  • Staircase flight
  • SHR & LMR head room slab
  • Drain cover slab
  • Half slab

05 · Plant & machinery

A one-stop plant fleet.

The P&M department is L&W's one-stop specialist for plant and machinery in the construction field, supplying projects with equipment across three families.

Lifting and access

  • Tower cranes up to 50T capacity
  • Twin and single cage builder hoists
  • Rope suspended platforms
  • Mast climbing platforms
  • Scissor lifts

Earth moving equipment

  • Backhoe loaders
  • Excavators
  • Skid steer loaders
  • Rollers

Lifting specialties

  • Mobile cranes
  • Crawler cranes
  • Gantry / EOT cranes
  • Pick and carry cranes
