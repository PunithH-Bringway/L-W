Profile

A Singapore builder,
made in India.

Read the full corporate profile

L&W Construction Private Limited is the Indian arm of Lee Kim Tah–Woh Hup, a partnership of two of Singapore's oldest construction houses. We came to Bengaluru in 2006, won our first contract from Ascendas in 2008, and have been handed the hardest briefs in Indian construction ever since.

We specialise in design and build: one team, one point of responsibility, from the first drawing to the day the keys change hands. High-rise residential, townships, commercial towers, IT parks, industrial complexes, malls and hotels, delivered as D&B, development or general contracts.

Vision

To be the leading and preferred contractor and business partner in India and the region, and the employer of choice.

Mission

To deliver excellent, reliable buildings with integrity, through trained, competent and responsible people.

Values

Safety. Integrity. Quality. Teamwork. Innovation. Growth.

Parent company

The names behind the name.

Our mark carries both parents: Lee Kim Tah's stepped tower and Woh Hup's knot. Their standards came with it.

The full parent company story

Woh Hup

Builder of Singapore's icons since 1927: Gardens by the Bay, Jewel Changi Airport, The Interlace, and Reflections at Keppel Bay, the largest residential development in Singapore's history. Their motto has not changed in a century: get it right the first time.

wohhup.com

Lee Kim Tah

A core contractor to the Singapore Government since the early 1980s, with nearly 140,000 HDB homes delivered, plus landmarks like Jurong Point and projects across China, Australia, the UK, Saudi Arabia, Thailand and Malaysia.

leekimtah.com
