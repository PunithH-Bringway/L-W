Built out step by step

The Indian group did not arrive fully formed. L&W Construction opened in Bengaluru in 2006 and spent its first two years on design, master planning and project management consultancy before winning its first construction contract in 2008. L&W Building Solutions followed in 2015 to take on interiors, landscape and BOQ contracts. Then came three incorporations in a single year, 2018: Invreco for specialised engineering works, Alexandrite ITP Development for IT parks and LWK Development for property. L&W Realty completed the family in 2020.

Each arm exists for one reason: a customer can hand the group a whole brief and deal with one accountable partner from design to delivery.
