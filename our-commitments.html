Our Commitments

Quality, environment, health and safety in one certified system, carried by partnership, site security and serious training.

Quality, environment, health & safety

L&W Construction Private Limited is committed to maintaining high levels of client satisfaction through innovative and value-added services. On every site, we hold ourselves to the same standing commitments:

  • Deliver projects in a timely and expeditious manner.
  • Adhere to quality, environment, health and safety parameters.
  • Eliminate environmental pollution and health and safety hazards.
  • Improve continually in every activity that touches quality, the environment, health and safety.
  • Comply with all applicable laws, regulations and standards, and maintain a safe, healthy work environment supported by environmentally friendly and safe work practices that prevent work-related injury and ill health.
  • Consult workers and their representatives, and keep them participating in the system.

Our uncompromising commitment to high quality and safety standards, and to minimising environmental risk, is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 by Bureau Veritas.

The four pillars

What we hold ourselves to.

Quality, environment, health & safety

One discipline covers the way we build: on-time delivery, strict QEHS parameters, pollution and hazards eliminated, continual improvement, full legal compliance, and workers consulted at every step. Certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.

Partnership approach

We value the input of every project participant: architects, consultants, owners and their representatives. A spirit of partnership between all parties carries execution to the desired quality with optimal efficiency.

Security

Maximising site security is always a priority. Perimeter fencing protects against pilferage, while a dedicated security team supervises personnel and vehicle movement, materials conveyance and storage round the clock.

Training

Our people are our most valuable asset. We invest in relevant training, in-house and external, to reinforce the skills and competency of our workforce, and we develop our staff to their full potential for an ever-changing business environment.
