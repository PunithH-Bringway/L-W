Projects of Lee Kim Tah

The builder of nearly 140,000 HDB homes, and core contractor to the Singapore Government since the early 1980s.

A national builder

Lee Kim Tah has been building since the 1920s. Chosen as a core contractor by the Singapore Government in the early 1980s, the group went on to complete nearly 140,000 HDB flat units, housing a generation of Singaporeans. Through that same decade it introduced modern construction technology to Singapore, and it stands among the first few construction companies listed on the Singapore Exchange.

Today Lee Kim Tah works across real estate and construction, with investments and operations in Singapore, Australia, China, the United Kingdom and India, and completed projects in China, Australia, the UK, Saudi Arabia, Thailand and Malaysia.
