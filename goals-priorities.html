Where we are going

Goals & Priorities

Five long-term goals and five immediate priorities, each anchored by a line the whole company can remember.

Long-term goals

The long game.

Five goals that shape how L&W grows: culture first, then innovation, digitalisation, hands-on training and partners who share the standard.

Goal 01

Cultural alignment through Build-to-Grow

Every colleague must be aligned to L&W's culture and way of working. That alignment is what keeps raising the bar on our performance, and the Build-to-Grow training programme is how we achieve it.

“People without the knowledge of their past history, origin and culture is like a tree without roots”

Goal 02

Innovation & improvement

Through continuous innovation and improvement, we will deliver higher levels of performance and superior quality services to our clients, consistently.

“If you always do what you always did, you will always get what you always got”

Goal 03

Digitalisation

Through digitalisation, we will execute key processes in effective and efficient ways, collaborate faster and gain quick visibility of key insights.

“It is not the strongest of the species that survives, nor the most intelligent that survives. It is the one that is the most adaptable to change”

Goal 04

Touch & feel experience centre

We will set up a modern touch and feel experience centre, developing our workforce with the latest training set-up.

“Seeing is believing, but feeling is the truth”

Goal 05

Continuous engagement of partners

Partners play a major role in our success. We engage and develop them continuously, through regular engagements, so they align with L&W's culture and priorities.

“Alone we can do so little; together we can do so much”

Immediate priorities

What cannot wait.

Five priorities in force today, starting with the one on our letterhead: building with integrity.

Priority 01

Building with Integrity

'Building with Integrity' must be followed in both letter and spirit. Strictly no exceptions.

“Integrity is doing the right thing even when no one is watching”

Priority 02

Keeping it simple and short

Keep your communications simple and to the point.

“Keep it simple and focus on what matters”

Priority 03

Time-bound action

Escalations raised to heads of department must be resolved swiftly.

“Without a sense of urgency, desire loses its value”

Priority 04

Developing the second level

Every leader has the responsibility to develop more leaders from the people working with them. Developing the second level is a must.

“True leaders don't create followers, they create more leaders”

Priority 05

Sustainable development

As a responsible contractor, L&W is committed to measures that minimise negative impact on the environment and reduce our greenhouse gas emissions. A holistic sustainability plan is in the works.

“The greatest threat to our planet is the belief that someone else will save it”
