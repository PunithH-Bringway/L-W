Who we are

Corporate Profile

The Indian arm of two of Singapore's oldest construction houses, building for the hardest briefs in the country since 2006.

Our roots

L&W Construction Private Limited is a 100 percent subsidiary of Lee Kim Tah–Woh Hup Pte Ltd of Singapore, itself a 50:50 joint venture between Lee Kim Tah Pte. Ltd. and Woh Hup Holdings Pte. Ltd., two of Singapore's oldest construction companies, each past its 90-year landmark.

Our journey

We started business in 2006 in Bengaluru, first building for our own property development projects. Two years of design, master planning and project management consultancy taught us the market from the inside. In 2008, Ascendas awarded us our first construction contract: Phase III of their Taramani campus in Chennai. We have not stopped since.

Our status

Today L&W runs regional offices across the South and West of India with a dedicated workforce of 1,200 professionals and over 5,000 workers in building and civil works, and stands among the leading construction companies in the country.

Our presence

As a specialist in design and build construction, we give our customers a single point of responsibility from design to delivery. More than twenty satisfied customers, most of them multinational companies and leading developers, have partnered with L&W to take their business across India.

Our services

Our construction focus covers high-rise residential buildings, townships, commercial buildings, IT parks, industrial complexes, shopping malls and hotels, delivered as D&B projects, development and GC contracts. Through our subsidiaries we also provide interiors, landscape, BOQ civil contracts, MEP, façade, precast, structural steel, plant & machinery and property development.
