L&W Construction

Civil, engineering and construction for the country's most demanding briefs, delivered as design & build projects, development and general contracts.

Civil, engineering & construction

L&W Construction Private Limited stands among the leading construction companies in India. Our construction focus includes, but is not limited to:

  • High-rise residential buildings
  • Townships
  • Commercial buildings
  • IT parks
  • Industrial complexes
  • Shopping malls
  • Hotels

Every one of them carries the same three commitments to our clients: superior quality, safety and timelines.

How we contract

Clients across India engage us in the form that suits their project best.

  • D&B projects
  • Development
  • GC contracts

Superior quality

Built to the specification our clients sign for, and finished to the standard they expect.

Safety

Safe delivery for everyone on site, on every site, for the full life of the programme.

Timelines

Programmes kept and handovers met, from the first pour to the last inspection.

The next front

Infrastructure.

India has one of the largest road networks in the world, spanning over 5.5 million kilometres, and transport keeps growing as connectivity improves between cities, towns and villages across the country. The nation is set to spend USD 1.4 trillion on infrastructure over the next five years.

With that investment coming, L&W has decided to enter infrastructure works. These are the fields where we intend to build:

  • Highways
  • Bridges
  • Railways
  • Runways
  • Test tracks
  • Land development
