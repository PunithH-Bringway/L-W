Giving back

CSR & Community

We make it our duty to better lives in the community. Over the years that duty has taken many forms: medical equipment, flood relief, classrooms and education support.

Community initiatives

Where we have stepped in.

Medical equipment donation · Chikkaballapur

A donation of medical equipment for Chikkaballapur, putting working tools into the hands of local healthcare.

Kaleidoscope

A community initiative held in March 2020 under the L&W CSR programme.

Flood relief

Relief activities for communities hit by flooding, organised and carried out by our teams.

Education enhancement

Programmes that strengthen education in the community, one of the longest-running strands of our outreach.

Classrooms for a government school

Construction of classrooms for a government school. We build for a living, so this is the most natural way we give.

CSR marathon

Our people on the road for the ACT Marathon, running as part of the L&W CSR programme.
